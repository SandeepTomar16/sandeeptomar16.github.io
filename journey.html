Step 1 of 5

Listen

Start by asking the people who will use it.

At Phatse I interviewed more than 100 customers and event organisers and set the roadmap from what they told me. NPS rose 30%. At K2 I spoke to learners after they bought a course, to find out why they bought it and where they got stuck.

Next: Decide

Step 2 of 5

Decide

Choose what to build, and what to leave out.

Interviews give you a long list. Deciding is cutting it down, and doing it with a price and a budget in mind.

At K2, for each course

  • Demand research and a defined customer
  • Build in-house or hire an outside expert
  • A price from competitor analysis, inside the budget

At Phatse

  • Roadmap set from 100+ interviews
  • A 20% commission on each booking
  • An instant reward for referring a friend

Next: Build

Step 3 of 5

Build

Write down what it should do, then work with the people who build it.

At K2 I specified an AI workflow that flags which course content needs updating after each Salesforce release. I also specified a learner dashboard that helped lift course completion by 24%. My engineering team built both. At Phatse I led a team of 18.
