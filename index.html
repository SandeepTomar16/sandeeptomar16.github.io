Every Salesforce release meant combing through thousands of lines of release notes by hand to find what each course needed.

Process

How I run a course, end to end.

The same four steps carried me through all 57 courses.

Research the demand

Find the next course worth building, define who it is for, and check the competition and pricing.

Plan and staff it

Decide whether to build in-house or with an external SME, then plan and budget it in Jira.

Launch it

Publish on the LMS and write a marketing brief so the launch lands with the right audience.

Keep it current

Track every release and update what changed, with AI-assisted flagging to make that fast.

Principles

What I care about.

Accuracy after launch day

A course is only as good as its latest update. Maintenance deserves a process, not an afterthought.

Less manual glue work

If people are re-reading thousands of lines to find a handful of changes, there is a better way to do it.

Listen after the sale

Learners tell you what they actually came for, and where they got stuck. I ask.

Plan inside the budget

Clear scope, honest estimates, and partners who get paid on time and know what is expected.
