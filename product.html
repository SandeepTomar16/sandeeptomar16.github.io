Product leadership

What I do as a product leader

Product work falls into four areas: understanding customers, setting strategy, executing, and leading people. Here is what each one means, and where I have done it.

I have owned products from the first customer conversation to years of maintenance, as a founder and as a product manager.

Customer insight

Knowing who the customer is, what they need, and what the numbers say.

Voice of the customer

Talking to customers and clients directly and turning what they say into decisions.

  • Interviewed 100+ customers and event organisers and 30+ vendors, and observed 20+ parties (Phatse)
  • Spoke with learners after purchase about why they bought and where they got stuck (K2U)
  • Talked with B2B clients, about 20% of the K2U business, to understand what their teams needed
  • Used the premium learner question queue as a live signal of where courses lost people

Data and metrics

Setting the right numbers and using them to decide.

  • Defined KPIs for every product at launch and tracked them after
  • Tracked funnels and drop-off in Mixpanel; ran A/B tests and kept what performed better
  • Worked out unit economics: about 600 rupees to acquire a customer against 4,800 rupees in commission (8:1)
  • Prepared post-launch projections for new courses

User experience

Designing products that are easy to use.

  • Wrote personas, customer journey maps and flow charts (Phatse)
  • Made wireframes and prototypes with design and engineering (SearchFinn, K2U)
  • Designed the learner progress dashboard that helped lift completion 24%

Product strategy

Deciding what to build, why, and how it moves the business.

Vision and roadmap

Setting direction and the order of work.

  • Set the product vision and roadmap for Phatse as founder
  • Prioritised the K2U course backlog with RICE
  • Sized demand, competitors and pricing for each new course

Business outcomes

Tying product work to revenue, retention and growth.

  • 600K rupees in bookings in Phatse’s first 2 months
  • A referral reward that drove 50% month-over-month growth
  • Framed the K2U dashboard as a retention and lifetime-value lever, not only a feature

Strategic bets

Choosing where to play next.

  • Launched a second business line, the Shopify brand Boxyt
  • Decided which courses to build in-house and which with external experts
  • Proposed a predictive retention and lifetime-value feature at K2U

Execution

Turning decisions into shipped, working products.

Requirements

Clear specs that teams can build from.

  • Wrote PRDs, user stories and requirements
  • Specified the release-notes workflow and the revenue-sharing tool for engineering

Delivery

Getting it built and shipped.

  • Launched about 45 courses at K2U, within approved budgets
  • Ran work in Jira with kanban, agile or waterfall to fit the project
  • Worked with engineers on a React rebuild that cut load time by 40% (SearchFinn)

Release management

Shipping updates on a predictable rhythm.

  • Ran course updates for three Salesforce releases a year
  • Delegated release-note reviews and update projects to the content team and project managers
  • Reviewed the work, then pushed the updates live on the learning platform myself
  • Cut update cycles from 80-90 days to 30-45

Quality and service

Making sure it works, and keeps working.

  • Tested new Salesforce apps in dev and UAT environments and flagged bugs before release
  • Ran a 24-hour SLA on premium learner questions
  • Kept courses current through every Salesforce release

Launch and go-to-market

Getting the product in front of customers.

  • Wrote launch marketing briefs for new courses
  • Ran live bootcamps on LinkedIn and YouTube: 450+ attended, about 17% bought within 24 hours
  • Ran Google Ads and email campaigns in Mailchimp and Sendinblue

Leading people

Getting results through teams, partners and leadership.

Team leadership

Building and running teams.

  • Led 18 at K2U (15 content, 3 project managers) and 18 at Phatse (8 engineers, 5 sales and marketing, 5 interns)
  • Delegated projects to project managers and the content team, and held a clear bar before anything went live
  • Led 60 people across a 7-ship fleet at Carnival UK
  • Cut onboarding from about 3 weeks to about 1

Stakeholders and partners

Bringing many groups to one plan.

  • Managed about 8 external experts, their revenue share and invoices
  • Worked across engineering, content, marketing and management
  • Built a vendor and organiser network at Phatse

Managing up

Winning support from leadership.

  • Pitched the release-notes AI workflow to management and got it approved
  • Took the learner dashboard through approval to build
  • Kept delivery going when the team shrank from 18 to 5 after an acquisition

My first 90 days in a new role

Days 1 to 30

Listen and map

Talk to customers, the team and leadership. Learn the numbers that matter and where they come from. Map every product, owner and date.

Days 31 to 60

Focus

Agree the one or two metrics to move. Set up the weekly rhythm, the roadmap and clear owners. Ship the first quick wins.

Days 61 to 90

Deliver and systemise

Ship the first bigger change and measure it. Write the procedures, automate the repeat work, and report results to leadership.
